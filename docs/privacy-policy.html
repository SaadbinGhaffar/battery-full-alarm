Privacy Policy

For the mobile application Charged: Battery Full Alarm (Android package com.bigbyte.charged)
Last updated: 7 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BigByte ("we", "us", "our") handles information in connection with the Charged: Battery Full Alarm mobile application (the "App"). By installing or using the App, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Summary

  • We do not require you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email, or phone number.
  • Your battery readings and in-app settings stay on your device; we never receive or store them.
  • The App shows ads through Google AdMob, which collects certain data (such as advertising identifiers) to serve and measure ads.
  • Where required by law, the App asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Data processed only on your device

The App reads your device's battery level and charging state to know when your target charge is reached, and stores your preferences (such as the charge target and whether sound, vibration, and notifications are enabled). This information is kept locally on your device using the operating system and local storage. It is not transmitted to us or to any third party, and it is removed when you uninstall the App.

2.2 Data collected by our advertising partner (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display banner and native ads. To provide and measure advertising, Google and its partners may automatically collect and process:

  • Advertising identifiers and other device identifiers;
  • IP address and approximate (non-precise) location derived from it;
  • Device information (such as device model, operating system version, and language);
  • Ad interaction and app usage related to ads (such as ads shown and tapped);
  • Diagnostics such as crash and performance data from the ads SDK.

This data is collected directly by Google under its own privacy policy. We do not control, and do not separately receive, this raw data. Learn more: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. How the information is used

  • To detect when your phone reaches its charge target and alert you (on-device data).
  • To remember your settings between sessions (on-device data).
  • To display, personalize (with consent), cap, and measure advertising, and to detect invalid activity and fraud (handled by Google AdMob).

4. Advertising and your choices

On first launch, where applicable law (such as the EU/UK GDPR) requires it, the App presents a consent request using Google's User Messaging Platform. Your choice controls whether you receive personalized or non-personalized ads. You can review or change your choice at any time via Settings → Ads & Privacy → Privacy options inside the App (shown where a consent choice applies).

You can also limit ad personalization at the device level:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads, where you can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalization.

5. Permissions the App uses

6. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. Data collected for advertising is shared with Google (as our advertising provider) and processed under Google's policies. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect legal rights.

7. Data retention

On-device data persists until you clear the App's data or uninstall the App. Data collected by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's retention practices.

8. Security

Data transmitted by the ads SDK is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we and our partners take reasonable measures to protect information.

9. Children's privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the EU/UK GDPR or the California CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising. Because we do not hold an account for you, you can exercise most choices directly through the in-app privacy options and your device's advertising settings described above. For requests involving data held by Google, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy. You may also contact us using the details below.

11. International transfers

Our advertising partner may process data in countries other than your own. Where applicable, such transfers are carried out under appropriate safeguards as described in Google's policies.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will revise the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes, provide notice within the App or on this page. Continued use of the App after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
