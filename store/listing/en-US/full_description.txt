CHARGED is a battery full alarm that rings the moment your phone reaches the charge level you choose: 80%, 90% or 100%. Unplug on time, stop overcharging and protect your battery health.

Leaving a phone on the charger at 100% for hours wears out a lithium-ion battery faster. A charge limit of 80% or 90% is gentler and helps the battery last longer. CHARGED gives you a loud full charge alert as soon as you hit your target, so you know exactly when to unplug.

★ A BATTERY FULL ALARM YOU WON'T MISS
• Loud, looping charging alarm the instant your battery reaches your target
• Sound, vibration and a notification, even when the phone is on silent
• Big "CHARGE COMPLETE!" screen with a one-tap STOP button
• Unplug the charger and the alarm stops by itself

★ SET YOUR CHARGE LIMIT: 80%, 90% OR 100%
Choose when the battery alarm rings. An 80% or 90% charge limit reduces battery wear. 100% alerts you at full charge.

★ WORKS IN THE BACKGROUND
The background alarm keeps watching your battery level while the app is closed or the screen is off, with a small notification while it watches. Stop the alarm right from the notification, even on the lock screen.

Neon synthwave grid, pixel fonts and an arcade-style battery monitor. A charging alarm doesn't have to look boring.

★ SIMPLE AND PRIVATE

★ GREAT FOR
